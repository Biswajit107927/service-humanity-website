## Who we are

Service to Humanity Foundation is a registered charitable organisation based in
Odisha, India. We nurture and educate orphaned and deprived children — building
independent, honest and confident human beings.

## Our founding

The foundation was established on **January 30, 1981** by **Dr. Sashikanta
Acharya** (PhD, TU Berlin).

## Our mission

Our mission is to provide love, care, shelter, education and values to children
who have lost their families or who come from very difficult circumstances.

## What we believe

Every child deserves a childhood, a safe home, an education and someone who
believes in them. We work to build that foundation one child at a time.

## Where we work

We are primarily active in the state of **Odisha**, India, where we operate
**6 orphanages / children's homes** and community-based education support
programmes, currently caring for **more than 50 children**.

## Legal status

The organisation is registered under the **Juvenile Justice (JJ) Act of 2000**
and holds **80G tax-exempt** status in India.

## Why "Service to Humanity"

The name reflects the simple belief at the heart of the organisation: service to
another human being is the most meaningful work there is.
