## Who the foundation supports

We support **orphaned, abandoned and deprived children** — boys and girls who,
without intervention, would not have access to safe shelter, food, education or
adult guidance.

## How children come to us

Children reach us through local authorities, child welfare committees, schools,
community members and partner organisations. We follow a careful intake process
to make sure every child is placed in a safe and caring environment.

## How long children stay with us

Children live with us until they complete their education and are prepared to
stand on their own. Our aim is to walk with each child from childhood to
independence.

## What children receive

- Safe, clean, family-like accommodation
- Nutritious food and healthcare
- School enrolment, books, uniforms and tuition support
- Extra classes and mentorship
- Values, life skills and emotional support
- Guidance for higher education and employment
