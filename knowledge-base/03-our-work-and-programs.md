## Children's home

The heart of the work is a residential children's home where children live in a
safe, caring environment with dedicated caregivers.

## Education

Every child is enrolled in school. We provide uniforms, books, stationery,
tuition fees, extra coaching and a quiet space to study.

## Healthcare

Children receive regular health check-ups and medical care when needed.

## Life skills and values

Beyond academics, we teach honesty, hard work, empathy and independence —
the values that help children become confident adults.

## Alumni and independence

When our young people grow up we continue to stand behind them — helping with
higher education, vocational training and first jobs so they can build
independent lives.
