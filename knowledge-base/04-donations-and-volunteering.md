## How to donate

Donations help us provide food, shelter, education, healthcare and care for the
children. The website's **Donate** section shows the donation options currently
available.

## What your donation supports

- Nutritious food for the children
- School fees, books and uniforms
- Medical care and hygiene supplies
- Maintenance of the children's home
- Special needs and celebrations that make every child feel valued

## Sponsorship

Supporters can also enquire about sponsoring a child's education or monthly
care. Contact the foundation through the website's contact details for current
sponsorship options.

## Volunteering

We welcome volunteers who can donate time, skills or resources. Teachers,
tutors, mentors, doctors and skilled professionals have all contributed over the
years. Please reach out through the contact details on the website.

## Receipts and transparency

Donors can request receipts / certificates and view the foundation's
certificates and compliance documents in the **Certificates** section of the
website.
