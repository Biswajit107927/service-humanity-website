## Registration and legal status

Service to Humanity Foundation is a registered non-profit organisation operating
in India.

## Certificates available on the website

The **Certificates** section of the website publishes official documents,
including:

- CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) documentation
- Form 10AB approval order documents

## Why we publish certificates

Publishing our registration and approval documents reflects our commitment to
transparency, good governance and public accountability.
