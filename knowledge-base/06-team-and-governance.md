## Our founder

Service to Humanity Foundation was established on January 30, 1981 by
**Dr. Sashikanta Acharya** (PhD, TU Berlin). His vision and picture are
featured in the website's **Our Founder** section.

## Our homes

The foundation runs **6 orphanages / children's homes in Odisha**, currently
caring for more than 50 orphaned and deprived children.

## Our team

The foundation is run by a dedicated team of trustees, staff members and
caregivers who put the children's well-being first.

## Our people on the website

The website's **Our Team** section introduces the people who lead and support
the work of the foundation.

## Governance values

We are committed to honesty, transparency, careful use of donations and full
accountability to the children, families and supporters we serve.
