## Can I visit the foundation?

We are generally happy to share our work, but visits must be arranged in
advance. Contact us through the details on the website to request a visit.

## Can I sponsor a child?

Yes — please contact us through the website to ask about current sponsorship
opportunities.

## What kind of help do you need most?

Recurring donations, school-supply sponsorships and skilled volunteers (teachers,
tutors, mentors, doctors) are always valued.

## How can someone contact the foundation?

Please use the contact information and **Donate** section on the website. The
website is the best public channel to reach the team.
